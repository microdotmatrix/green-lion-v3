# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Green Lion staff manage a B2B manufacturing catalog and business operations through the authenticated admin section. Inbound samples are devices received from manufacturers for evaluation before catalog listing.

## Product Purpose

Keep routine administration straightforward. For inbound samples, staff record a photo and basic specifications, track testing, and record an attributable final decision. Catalog creation remains a separate task.

## Brand Personality

Practical, clear, consistent. The user confirmed that inbound samples should use the existing admin style.

## Anti-references

Avoid introducing a new visual system or decorative marketing patterns into the existing admin workflow. Follow existing tables, forms, navigation, and semantic feedback.

## Design Principles

- Reuse the established admin shell and components.
- Keep receiving a sample quick; detailed catalog specifications come later.
- Distinguish testing progress from the final approval decision.
- Make uploader and reviewer attribution visible and trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use accessible labels, keyboard controls, textual status indicators, and clear validation feedback, consistent with the user's confirmed direction.
